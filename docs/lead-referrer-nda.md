## 2. Background and Purpose

2.1 The Recipient has entered into a Lead Referrer Independent Contractor Agreement with FNC ("the Services Agreement").

2.2 In the course of performing the services under the Services Agreement, the Recipient will necessarily have access to confidential and proprietary information belonging to FNC, its clients, funders, and referral partners.

2.3 This Agreement governs the treatment, use, and protection of that confidential information.

## 3. Definition of Confidential Information

3.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all information, in any form, disclosed to or accessed by the Recipient, whether before, during, or after the term of the Services Agreement, including but not limited to:

- 3.1.1 **Client Information** — the identity, contact details, financial position, funding requirements, business affairs, and any personal information (as defined in POPIA) of any past, current, or prospective client of FNC;
- 3.1.2 **Funder Information** — the identity, terms, criteria, pricing, product structures, decisioning processes, and commercial arrangements of any funder or funding partner of FNC, including that funder display names in partner-facing surfaces may differ from underlying funder legal names;
- 3.1.3 **Referral Partner Information** — the identity, commission structure, tier allocation, and commercial arrangements of any referral partner of FNC;
- 3.1.4 **Commercial Information** — FNC's commission structures, tier tables, pricing, product mix, revenue figures, statement content, and any information relating to the financial performance or projections of FNC;
- 3.1.5 **Platform and System Information** — the design, functionality, workflows, features, data structures, code, and any technical or operational information relating to the FNC platform, including any credentials, access tokens, URLs, or configurations;
- 3.1.6 **Business Strategy** — any information relating to FNC's business plans, growth strategy, partnerships, product roadmap, hiring plans, or expansion;
- 3.1.7 **Lead Referrer Programme Information** — the identity, performance, commission history, tier, and any information relating to any other Lead Referrer or contractor of FNC;
- 3.1.8 Any information marked or communicated as confidential, or which by its nature would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

3.2 Confidential Information includes information in any form — written, verbal, electronic, visual, or otherwise — and whether or not marked "confidential".

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 Confidential Information does not include information that:

- 4.1.1 Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Recipient;
- 4.1.2 Was in the Recipient's lawful possession, without confidentiality restriction, prior to disclosure by FNC (as evidenced by written records);
- 4.1.3 Is independently developed by the Recipient without reference to or use of FNC's Confidential Information (as evidenced by written records);
- 4.1.4 Is lawfully received by the Recipient from a third party who has the right to disclose it without confidentiality restriction.

4.2 The exclusions in this clause do not apply to personal information as defined by POPIA, which remains subject to the obligations under POPIA and clause 8 of the Services Agreement in all circumstances.

## 5. Obligations of the Recipient

5.1 The Recipient undertakes and agrees:

- 5.1.1 To hold all Confidential Information in strictest confidence;
- 5.1.2 To use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing the Services under the Services Agreement, and for no other purpose whatsoever;
- 5.1.3 Not to disclose, communicate, publish, copy, or make available Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of FNC;
- 5.1.4 Not to use Confidential Information for their own benefit or for the benefit of any third party, including for the purpose of soliciting clients, funders, or referral partners of FNC;
- 5.1.5 To take all reasonable steps to protect Confidential Information from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure, including the use of secure passwords, screen locks, and secure storage;
- 5.1.6 Not to remove, extract, download, screenshot, print, photograph, or otherwise reproduce Confidential Information outside of the FNC platform except as strictly required for the performance of the Services;
- 5.1.7 Not to store Confidential Information on personal devices, personal cloud storage, personal email accounts, or any third-party systems not authorised by FNC;
- 5.1.8 To immediately notify FNC of any suspected or actual unauthorised disclosure, loss, or compromise of Confidential Information;
- 5.1.9 To comply with all applicable data protection laws, including POPIA, in respect of any personal information forming part of the Confidential Information.

5.2 The Recipient acknowledges that the disclosure of Confidential Information to family members, friends, or associates, including through social media, WhatsApp groups, or informal conversation, is a breach of this Agreement.

5.3 The Recipient shall not make any public statement, post on social media, or make any comment to media concerning FNC, its clients, funders, referral partners, or business affairs without the prior written consent of FNC.

## 6. Legally Compelled Disclosure

6.1 In the event the Recipient is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information by court order, subpoena, or statutory requirement:

- 6.1.1 The Recipient shall promptly notify FNC in writing prior to disclosure (unless prohibited by law) to allow FNC to seek a protective order;
- 6.1.2 The Recipient shall disclose only the minimum Confidential Information legally required;
- 6.1.3 The Recipient shall use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment of the disclosed information.

## 7. Return and Destruction

7.1 Upon termination of the Services Agreement, or at any time on written request by FNC, the Recipient shall:

- 7.1.1 Immediately cease all use of Confidential Information;
- 7.1.2 Return to FNC all Confidential Information in tangible form (documents, media, devices) in their possession or control;
- 7.1.3 Permanently delete all Confidential Information in electronic form from all systems, devices, cloud storage, and email accounts under their control;
- 7.1.4 Provide FNC with a written certificate confirming compliance with this clause within seven (7) days of termination or request.

7.2 The obligation to return or destroy does not extinguish the ongoing confidentiality obligations under this Agreement.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of FNC (or, as applicable, its clients, funders, or referral partners).

8.2 Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence, right, title, or interest in any Confidential Information, intellectual property, or trademarks of FNC, save the limited right to use Confidential Information for the purpose of performing the Services.

## 9. Duration

9.1 This Agreement commences on the date of signature and continues for the full duration of the Services Agreement.

9.2 The Recipient's confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive termination of the Services Agreement and shall continue:

- 9.2.1 Indefinitely in respect of personal information as defined by POPIA and in respect of trade secrets;
- 9.2.2 For a period of five (5) years post-termination in respect of all other Confidential Information.

## 10. Breach and Remedies

10.1 The Recipient acknowledges that:

- 10.1.1 Confidential Information is highly valuable to FNC;
- 10.1.2 Any breach of this Agreement is likely to cause immediate and irreparable harm to FNC for which monetary damages may be inadequate;
- 10.1.3 FNC shall be entitled to seek urgent injunctive relief (including an interdict) in addition to damages and any other remedy available at law, without the need to prove actual damage or to post security.

10.2 In the event of breach, FNC's remedies shall be cumulative and not exclusive.

10.3 The Recipient shall be liable for all costs (including legal costs on an attorney-and-own-client scale) incurred by FNC in enforcing this Agreement.

## 11. Warranties

11.1 The Recipient warrants that:

- 11.1.1 They have full legal capacity to enter into this Agreement;
- 11.1.2 They are not bound by any other confidentiality obligation that conflicts with this Agreement;
- 11.1.3 They will comply with all applicable laws, including POPIA, in the treatment of Confidential Information.

## 12. General

12.1 **Whole Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Services Agreement, constitutes the whole agreement between the Parties concerning confidentiality.

12.2 **Variation.** No variation shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 **No Waiver.** No indulgence granted by FNC shall constitute a waiver.

12.4 **Severability.** If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force.

12.5 **Assignment.** The Recipient may not assign or transfer this Agreement. FNC may assign this Agreement in whole or in part to any successor entity.

12.6 **Domicilium.** Each Party chooses the address stated in clause 1 as their *domicilium citandi et executandi*.

12.7 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

12.8 **Jurisdiction.** The Parties consent to the jurisdiction of the Magistrate's Court having jurisdiction, notwithstanding that the amount in dispute may exceed such jurisdiction.

12.9 **Electronic Execution.** The Parties consent to electronic execution of this Agreement under the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002.
