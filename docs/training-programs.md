## Shared delivery model

Training lives inside the existing CRM and uses versioned lessons, progress records, assessments, certificates, notifications and audit history.

Each lesson may contain:

- written material;
- a 3-8 minute owner-recorded or owner-approved YouTube video;
- a transcript or concise accessible summary;
- one practical scenario or exercise;
- a short knowledge check; and
- the content version and approval date.

An external video is not publishable until the owner has reviewed its accuracy, rights, advertisements, external links and consistency with FNC policy. Removing or changing a video must not erase historical completion records.

## Lead Referrer track

Source: Lead Referrer Training Material supplied on 2026-08-06.

### Curriculum

1. FNC purpose, story, values and broker role.
2. Product identification: working capital, PO, invoice, trade, asset, bridge, contract/cession and equity/capital raising.
3. What makes a good lead and how to spot warning signs.
4. Professional conversations without promising outcomes or quoting fees/rates.
5. POPIA, confidentiality, fraud escalation and consent to be contacted.
6. Structured lead submission in the CRM.
7. Handover: what the referrer may see and when client contact must stop.
8. Commission/tier concepts using only the effective owner-approved schedule.
9. Values, reputation, long-term development and practical scenarios.

### Non-negotiable boundary assessment

A Lead Referrer must demonstrate that they will not:

- collect or upload client paperwork;
- send or discuss a client mandate;
- quote fees, rates or funding terms;
- reveal or contact funders;
- negotiate or bind FNC; or
- continue client contact after accepted handover.

### Completion

- Passing score: 75%.
- All boundary questions must be correct even if the aggregate score passes.
- Certificate: Certified FNC Lead Referrer.
- Validity: 12 months, followed by an annual refresher.
- Activation also requires the approved agreements and commission schedule described in `lead-referrer-role.md`.

## Partner/Doctor track

Partners/Doctors complete the Contractor baseline, then the following advanced plan.

### Phase 1 - Governance and access

- Difference between owner, Partner/Doctor, Contractor and Lead Referrer authority.
- Attributable-client and deal access only.
- Real-funder, economics and sensitive-data visibility rules.
- Audit, conflict-of-interest, confidentiality and escalation obligations.

### Phase 2 - Deal quality

- Review qualification and product fit before submission.
- Review the Smart Documentation Checklist without bypassing owner verification.
- Detect missing, expired, mismatched, altered or illegible paperwork.
- Use Tasks and escalation rather than off-platform follow-up records.

### Phase 3 - Mandates and client communication

- Send only an owner-approved mandate with the fee selected for that deal.
- Never change or quote the success fee independently.
- Track Mandate, Authority/Consent and FNC countersignature status.
- Do not approach funders until the database submission gate is satisfied.

### Phase 4 - Pipeline and people oversight

- Manage assigned Contractor performance and deal handoffs.
- Monitor ageing, stalled paperwork, rejections and compliance risk.
- Keep Lead Referrer boundaries intact after handover.
- Escalate exceptions to the owner with a complete audit trail.

### Phase 5 - Advanced product and funder fit

- Category-level fit across the current 49+ active funders, 7 VC and 8 private-equity relationships.
- Complex trade, contract, equity and capital-raising cases.
- No unauthorised disclosure of funder identities, contacts or commercial arrangements.

### Completion

- Prerequisite: current Contractor certification or owner-approved equivalent.
- Pass mark: 85%.
- Mandatory perfect score on fee authority, submission gates, funder confidentiality and POPIA scenarios.
- Practical owner review of one sample deal package.
- Certificate: Certified FNC Partner/Doctor, valid for 12 months.

## Owner controls and reporting

The owner can:

- assign tracks and lesson versions by role;
- approve, retire and replace videos;
- manage question banks without exposing answers;
- view completion, scores, retries and certification expiry;
- require a refresher after a material product, legal or workflow change; and
- block role activation or higher-risk permissions until the applicable gate is satisfied.

The CRM must report completion by role, overdue training, assessment failure hotspots, certificates expiring in 30/14/7 days and the exact content version each person completed.
