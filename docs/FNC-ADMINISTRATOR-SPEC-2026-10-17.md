# Administrator (Second-in-Charge) and booking hours: owner specification (17 October 2026)

Saved from the owner's messages of 17 Oct 2026. This is the agreed scope; build it in batches (below) and smoke-test each.
Corrections from the owner are already folded in (payslips, success-fee invoices hidden, Agent Commission Notice).

## The role

New staff role **Administrator (Second-in-Charge)**, separate from Switchboard Assistant and Sales Coordinator.
Second after the Director (Thapelo) and in charge when the Director is unavailable.

## 1. Funders
The Administrator CANNOT see funder names or funder details. Only the Director sees funders.

## 2. Booking hours and working hours
- The Administrator manages the Director's booking hours, calendar, meetings and presentations, and writes meeting notes.
- The Administrator manages working hours for all internal staff.
- Every booking created, changed or cancelled notifies the Director and CCs the Administrator.

### Booking windows on the Director's diary (owner, 17 Oct)
| Time (Africa/Johannesburg, weekdays) | Who may book |
|---|---|
| 08:30–14:00 | Administrator, Sales Coordinator |
| 14:00–17:00 | Agents, sub-contractors, clients, Team Leaders, partners |
| 17:00–19:00 (after hours) | Anyone, with a written reason; Administrator or Director approves |
| Outside your own window ("Important") | Anyone (staff and team) may request it; Administrator or Director approves |
| Any time | The Director |

Weekdays only; public holidays the owner enters are closed. Nothing after 19:00.
**Assumption to confirm:** after-hours requests need a reason AND approval (the owner said "must have a reason";
approval by the Administrator or Director is applied to both important and after-hours requests).

## 3. Required documents and the R100 reward
- The Administrator sees each client's required-documents checklist.
- When the Director is unavailable, the Administrator can approve documents; that approval releases the agent's R100 reward.
- Log who approved and when.

## 4. Commission flow (deal fully paid by the client)
Director → Administrator → Sales Coordinator → agent; agent submits invoice to HR → HR → Administrator → Director;
Director approves and pays, uploads proof of payment → Administrator → HR → agent. Each step shows a status so everyone
can see where the invoice is.

**4a.** What the Director sends the Administrator is an **Agent Commission Notice**: client name, deal reference and the
agent's commission amount only. It must NOT show the client success-fee amount, the success-fee invoice or the funder.

## 5. Sales
Anything sales-related is handled by the Sales Coordinator and the Administrator.

## 6. Outbound leads routing
Outbound lead → Sales Coordinator, who checks with the team whether it belongs to anyone → assign to that person; if no
one, move to "New Leads" → Administrator → Director.

## 7. Payslips
The Administrator prepares and checks payslips and can see staff salaries. Flow: Administrator → HR → staff member.

## 8. Notices and forms
The Administrator creates notice forms with written notes for internal staff, sub-agents and Team Leaders. A notice can
be marked "Needs attention"; recipients must acknowledge it.

## 9. Internal messages
- Sub-agents can message the Sales Coordinator; the Director is CC'd.
- Every internal message the Sales Coordinator sends or receives automatically CCs the Director.
- The Administrator uses internal CRM mail with all staff and HR.

## 10. Other Administrator tasks
- Sees and oversees the Sales Coordinator's and Switchboard Assistants' tasks, and assigns tasks. Assigned tasks appear on
  the staff member's landing page to accept or decline.
- Creates and manages admin-fee invoices.
- **Client success-fee invoices: hidden completely** (not view-only). Only the Director creates, edits and sees them.
- Does verifications and handles stationery.

## 11. Daily and weekly routine
- Director's daily briefing 07:30–08:00: today's bookings, open tasks, urgent items.
- End-of-day report: files opened, documents received, leads assigned, tasks outstanding.
- Friday report on progress toward 60 files a month: files opened, admin fees paid, documents complete, deals in progress
  (no success-fee amounts).

## 12. Client files and admin fees
- Open new client files after consultation, each with a file number.
- Issue admin-fee invoices and receipts; reconcile Yoco card and EFT payments against invoices daily.
- Follow up missing required documents at 3 and 7 days, logging each follow-up.
- Every client signs the POPIA consent form before their documents are received.

## 13. Staff management
- Attendance, late-coming and leave requests (approve or escalate); leave calendar.
- Overdue tasks flagged on the staff member's landing page.
- Oversee the switchboard: call logs, missed calls, callback lists.
- Coordinate training and onboarding days with HR (venue, materials, attendance register).

## 14. Agents and sub-agents
- With HR, keep the agent onboarding tracker current: contracts, NDA, iFacts consent, headshots, ID copies.
- Agent register: active, inactive, pending agents and their Team Leaders.
- Track R100 document rewards owed and paid.

## 15. Office and finance admin
Petty cash (slip per spend, monthly reconciliation) · stationery, supplies, stamps, receipt books with reorder alerts ·
office bills with due dates and reminders · compliance diary reminders to the Director (EMP201 by the 7th, UIF, VAT once
registered, CIPC annual return) · office visitor log.

## 16. Records and CRM quality
Weekly check that client and lead records are complete, de-duplicated and correctly statused · file signed documents in
the right client or staff folder · keep letter, form and notice templates current.

## 17. Escalations
Anything not resolved within 24 hours is escalated to the Director with a note. Client or agent complaints are logged,
acknowledged the same day and escalated to the Director.
