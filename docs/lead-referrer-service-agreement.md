## 2. Definitions and interpretation

In this Agreement:

- **Approved Platform** means the FNC CRM and any other system or channel expressly approved in writing by FNC.
- **Commission Schedule** means the separately versioned and approved commercial annexure assigned to the Lead Referrer at signature.
- **Confidential Information** has the meaning in the accompanying Lead Referrer Non-Disclosure Agreement.
- **Lead** means prospect information lawfully collected and submitted through the Approved Platform for possible funding assistance.
- **Personal Information**, **Operator**, **Processing** and **Responsible Party** have the meanings given in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("**POPIA**").
- **Services** means only the services described in clause 4.

Headings assist reading and do not affect interpretation. References to legislation include lawful amendments and replacements. If a due date falls on a non-business day, performance is due on the next business day.

## 3. Independent-contractor relationship

3.1 The Parties intend an independent-contractor relationship. Nothing creates employment, partnership, joint venture, fiduciary agency or authority to represent or bind FNC.

3.2 The Lead Referrer determines when and where to source Leads and ordinarily uses their own equipment and resources, subject to the Approved Platform, lawful compliance controls, training requirements and the service standards in this Agreement. There is no guaranteed work, fixed salary, retainer, minimum commission or employee benefit.

3.3 The Lead Referrer may perform lawful services for others, provided this does not misuse FNC information, create an undisclosed conflict, breach clause 12 or interfere with obligations already accepted under this Agreement.

3.4 The description of the relationship is not conclusive. The Parties must ensure that the relationship in practice remains consistent with an independent engagement. Nothing in this Agreement excludes any non-waivable right or obligation that applies by law if the factual relationship is classified differently.

3.5 The Lead Referrer must not present themselves as an employee, representative, financial adviser, authorised agent or officer of FNC and may not use FNC branding except in the exact form and context FNC approves in writing.

## 4. Services and handover

4.1 Subject to activation under clause 5, the Lead Referrer may:

- identify prospective business-funding clients;
- obtain lawful permission to submit the minimum required Lead details to FNC;
- submit complete and accurate Lead information through the Approved Platform;
- perform only the initial follow-up tasks expressly assigned through the Approved Platform; and
- complete required training, assessments and compliance acknowledgements.

4.2 Handover occurs when the Lead is successfully submitted and accepted by the Approved Platform. After handover, FNC controls qualification, document collection, client mandates, funder engagement, proposals, approval, funding, invoicing and collections.

4.3 The Lead Referrer must not:

- provide financial, credit, tax or legal advice;
- recommend a particular funder or product, negotiate or quote rates, fees or commercial terms, or promise approval or funding;
- bind FNC, sign for FNC or make a representation outside approved scripts;
- contact a funder, submit anything to a funder or access a funder system;
- collect, receive, store or transmit client supporting documents, bank credentials, money or payments;
- use personal email, messaging, cloud storage, spreadsheets or unapproved devices/channels for FNC business;
- access another user's Leads, economics, agreements or personal information; or
- continue client contact after handover unless a specific, lawful task is assigned through the Approved Platform.

4.4 If an activity could amount to regulated advice, an intermediary service or another licensed activity, the Lead Referrer must stop and refer it to FNC. Nothing in this Agreement authorises regulated activity, and FNC may restrict the Services pending regulatory advice.

## 5. Conditions before activation

The Lead Referrer may not perform role work until FNC confirms activation after all applicable gates are complete, including identity and contact verification, this Agreement and the current NDA signed, the current Commission Schedule acknowledged, POPIA/security and platform-use acknowledgements completed, mandatory training and assessment passed, and any required FNC countersignature recorded. Suspension or expiry of a required gate suspends role access without deleting historical records.

## 6. Lead standards and conflicts

6.1 Every Lead must be genuine, lawfully sourced, accurate to the Lead Referrer's knowledge and submitted with a record of the prospect's permission or another lawful basis approved by FNC.

6.2 The Lead Referrer must not buy scraped data, submit fabricated or duplicate Leads, impersonate a prospect, misstate consent, offer an undisclosed inducement or interfere with an existing FNC relationship.

6.3 The Lead Referrer must disclose any personal, family, business or financial interest that could influence a referral. FNC may reject, reassign or investigate a Lead where duplication, conflict, fraud, consent or attribution is disputed.

## 7. Commission and payment

7.1 The Lead Referrer is eligible only for commission stated in the **published Commission Schedule** assigned to them. The Schedule must identify its version, effective period, rates, calculation base, tier rules, payment source and precedence. Blank or draft schedules create no entitlement and cannot activate an account.

7.2 The CRM's locked attribution and commission records are the operational source of calculation, subject to correction of a proven error. A material schedule change applies prospectively through a new published version and required acknowledgement; it does not silently rewrite an earlier locked transaction.

7.3 Commission becomes **earned** and **payable** only when every event defined in the applicable Commission Schedule has occurred, including FNC's receipt of cleared funds where the Schedule requires it. Payment is made monthly in arrears with a statement, subject to lawful deductions, tax documentation and a valid payment profile.

7.4 The Lead Referrer must dispute a statement through the Approved Platform within **[insert approved dispute period]** after it is made available, identifying the transaction and reason. FNC must investigate and preserve the underlying attribution, calculation and payment evidence. Failure to dispute does not validate fraud or an arithmetical error that could not reasonably have been identified earlier.

7.5 A commission may be reversed or offset only for a clawback event defined in the approved Schedule or this clause, supported by recorded evidence and notice to the Lead Referrer. Permitted events are transaction reversal, refund, non-payment, duplicate payment, or material fraud/misrepresentation attributable to the Lead Referrer. No earned or settled record may be deleted; adjustments must be recorded as auditable entries.

7.6 The Lead Referrer is responsible for their tax registration and liabilities. FNC may withhold or report amounts where required by law. The Lead Referrer must notify FNC if VAT registration applies and provide a valid tax invoice where legally required. Nothing in this Agreement fixes or represents a VAT-registration threshold.

7.7 Except as expressly stated in a published Schedule, no advance, retainer, minimum guarantee, expense reimbursement or employee benefit is payable.

## 8. Personal information and security

8.1 For Personal Information processed solely on FNC's documented instructions, FNC is the Responsible Party and the Lead Referrer acts as an Operator or person under FNC's authority, as the facts and POPIA determine.

8.2 The Lead Referrer must:

- process only the minimum Personal Information necessary for the authorised Lead purpose and only through the Approved Platform;
- follow FNC's lawful written instructions, privacy notices, retention rules and security procedures;
- maintain the confidentiality, integrity and availability of Personal Information using appropriate technical and organisational safeguards;
- not copy, download, photograph, print, export, combine, sell, reuse or process Personal Information for an independent purpose;
- not appoint a sub-operator or transfer information outside approved systems or jurisdictions without FNC's prior written approval and required safeguards;
- ensure that any person acting under their authority is bound by confidentiality and uses the information only as authorised;
- immediately report any suspected loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, destruction, credential compromise or other security incident, preserve evidence and cooperate with containment, investigation and legally required notifications;
- assist FNC with reasonable data-subject requests, regulatory enquiries, audits and information-quality corrections; and
- on instruction or termination, return or securely delete information not required to be retained by law and certify completion if requested.

8.3 The Lead Referrer may not notify a data subject, regulator, media outlet or third party about a security compromise on FNC's behalf unless FNC or the law expressly requires it.

8.4 FNC may process the Lead Referrer's Personal Information for onboarding, verification, access control, contract administration, training, attribution, commission/payment, tax, fraud prevention, security, audit, legal claims and regulatory compliance, subject to applicable law and FNC's privacy notice.

## 9. Confidentiality

The Lead Referrer must sign and comply with the current **Lead Referrer Non-Disclosure Agreement**, which is incorporated into this Agreement. A confidentiality or security breach is a material breach and may justify immediate suspension while FNC investigates and protects affected persons and systems.

## 10. Intellectual property, platform records and personal information

10.1 FNC owns its platform, templates, processes, training, scripts, branding, compilations, database rights and work product created specifically for FNC to the extent permitted by law. The Lead Referrer assigns transferable intellectual-property rights in such commissioned work product to FNC upon creation and must sign reasonable confirmatory documents.

10.2 Personal Information is not treated as property merely because it is submitted as a Lead. Data-subject rights and FNC's POPIA duties remain unaffected. The Lead Referrer receives only a limited, revocable right to process authorised information for the Services.

10.3 FNC's CRM records attribution, status and commission evidence. The Lead Referrer may receive only the safe status and economic information authorised for their own Leads.

## 11. Training, conduct and monitoring

11.1 The Lead Referrer must complete mandatory training and assessments, keep credentials secure, follow approved scripts and policies, and maintain truthful records.

11.2 FNC may monitor and audit use of its systems for security, service quality, fraud prevention and compliance, subject to law and its privacy notices. Monitoring does not authorise control inconsistent with the intended independent relationship.

11.3 The Lead Referrer must comply with applicable anti-bribery, anti-fraud, consumer-protection, direct-marketing and sanctions requirements and must not offer or accept an improper benefit.

## 12. Protection of legitimate business interests

12.1 During this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** after termination, the Lead Referrer must not use FNC Confidential Information or a relationship acquired through the Services to:

- divert or solicit for competing funding-introduction services a client, active prospect, funder or referral partner with whom the Lead Referrer had material dealings or about whom they received non-public information during the preceding twelve months;
- refer elsewhere a Lead first identified for and validly submitted to FNC while FNC is actively processing it; or
- induce an FNC employee or contractor with whom the Lead Referrer worked materially to terminate their engagement.

12.2 This clause does not prohibit general advertising not targeted using FNC information, work for another business that does not involve prohibited conduct, or dealings based on a demonstrably pre-existing independent relationship disclosed to FNC.

12.3 Each restriction is separate and applies only to the extent reasonably necessary to protect FNC's confidential information, client/funder connections and workforce stability. A court may enforce a valid restriction to the maximum lawful extent, but this clause does not expand any restriction beyond the supplied attorney-approved twelve-month period.

## 13. Warranties and indemnity

13.1 The Lead Referrer warrants that they have legal capacity, have supplied accurate identity and onboarding information, are not breaching another obligation, will obtain Lead information lawfully, and will not knowingly submit false or misleading information.

13.2 To the extent permitted by law, the Lead Referrer indemnifies FNC against direct loss, third-party claims, penalties and reasonable costs caused by the Lead Referrer's fraud, wilful misconduct, unlawful data collection, unauthorised representations, material breach, or tax obligations personal to the Lead Referrer. FNC must take reasonable steps to mitigate loss and may not recover twice for the same loss.

13.3 Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limits a data subject's or regulator's rights.

## 14. Suspension, term and termination

14.1 This Agreement begins when both Parties have signed and FNC has countersigned where required. It continues until terminated.

14.2 Either Party may terminate without cause on **thirty (30) days'** written notice.

14.3 FNC may immediately suspend access where reasonably necessary to address suspected fraud, security compromise, unauthorised activity, regulatory risk or material breach. FNC must record the reason and give the Lead Referrer a reasonable opportunity to respond where circumstances permit.

14.4 FNC may terminate immediately by written notice for a material breach not capable of remedy, or not remedied within a reasonable stated period; fraud or dishonesty; serious confidentiality/privacy/security breach; unauthorised funder contact or regulated activity; material reputational misconduct connected to the Services; insolvency; or three consecutive months without a qualifying Lead, as stated in the supplied source.

14.5 On termination, access and authority end immediately; FNC property and information must be returned or deleted; and historical records, legal holds and accrued rights remain preserved. Commission for transactions closed and settled before termination remains payable under the applicable Schedule, subject to evidenced clawback. No Lead may be submitted after termination.

14.6 Clauses intended by their nature to survive—including confidentiality, privacy, intellectual property, restraints, accrued commission, audit evidence, liability, disputes and governing law—survive termination.

## 15. Notices and disputes

15.1 Each Party chooses its physical address in clause 1 as its *domicilium citandi et executandi*. Operational notices may also be sent to the verified email address recorded in the CRM, but formal service must comply with applicable law.

15.2 A Party must notify the other of an address change. The change takes effect when received and may not select an address outside South Africa without written agreement.

15.3 Before litigation, a dispute must be referred to authorised representatives for good-faith negotiation for fifteen business days, except where urgent relief, prescription protection, confidentiality, data security or unlawful conduct requires immediate action.

15.4 This Agreement is governed by South African law. The Parties consent to the jurisdiction of a Magistrate's Court having jurisdiction, notwithstanding that a claim may exceed its ordinary monetary jurisdiction, without preventing either Party from approaching another court with lawful jurisdiction.

## 16. General

16.1 This Agreement, its published Commission Schedule, the NDA and the listed signed acknowledgements form the whole agreement on the Services. If they conflict, the main Agreement prevails on role and legal terms, the Commission Schedule prevails only on expressly identified commercial calculations, and stricter lawful confidentiality/security protection prevails for protected information.

16.2 No amendment is effective unless recorded in a new written version signed by authorised Parties. The CRM may not silently mutate an executed document or apply a material change retroactively.

16.3 A failure or delay to enforce a right is not a waiver. Invalid provisions are severable. Neither Party may cede or delegate this Agreement without written consent, except that FNC may transfer it as part of a lawful business reorganisation on notice without reducing accrued rights.

16.4 The Lead Referrer has had a reasonable opportunity to read the Agreement, ask questions and obtain independent advice before signing.
